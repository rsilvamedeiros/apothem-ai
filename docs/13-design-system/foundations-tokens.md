# Design System Foundations and Tokens

**Status:** Foundation / Draft  
**Project:** APOTHEM AI  
**Canonical domain:** `apothemai.com.br`

APOTHEM visual language is geometric, precise and enterprise. Avoid generic AI neon, robot/circuit imagery and decorative complexity.

Token categories:
- neutral background/surface/text scales;
- one controlled brand accent family;
- semantic success/warning/danger/info;
- spacing grid;
- radii with restrained geometry;
- elevation/border hierarchy;
- motion durations/easing;
- data visualization tokens;
- typography scale.

The apothem/core-to-edge geometry may appear subtly in icons, loading states, diagrams and brand motion without reducing product usability.
