# Brand and Positioning

**Status:** Foundation / Draft  
**Project:** APOTHEM AI  
**Canonical domain:** `apothemai.com.br`

## Brand

**APOTHEM AI** derives from the geometric apothem: the segment connecting the center of a regular polygon to one of its edges. The metaphor is useful because the product places intelligence at the core while connecting it to operational edges of a business.

## Primary tagline

**Intelligence at the core.**

Brazilian communication may use **“Inteligência no centro da sua operação.”** without requiring a literal translation.

## Category language

Preferred category descriptors:

- Enterprise AI;
- Intelligent Automation;
- AI Agents for Business;
- Intelligence Layer for Business.

Avoid positioning the company as a “ChatGPT for companies” or an “AI chatbot agency”.

## Voice

APOTHEM should sound precise, mature, technical and business-aware. Avoid magic language, vague revolution claims, robot imagery and exaggerated autonomy.

Good message:

> Connect company knowledge, systems and policies to AI agents that can perform real work with controlled permissions.

Weak message:

> Revolutionize everything with the power of AI.

## Visual principle

The geometric core-to-edge metaphor should influence the logo, iconography, motion and diagrams. The aesthetic direction is enterprise, minimal and geometric rather than neon/cyberpunk.
