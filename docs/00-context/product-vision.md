# Product Vision

**Status:** Foundation / Draft  
**Project:** APOTHEM AI  
**Canonical domain:** `apothemai.com.br`

## North Star

> **Become the intelligence layer between businesses, their data, their systems and their operations.**

APOTHEM should not be positioned as another generic AI assistant. Its value is the controlled combination of context, tools and execution.

## Core product loop

### Understand
Build usable context from business data, documents, history, identity, policies and current task state.

### Connect
Provide controlled interfaces to systems such as databases, ERP, CRM, email, storage, SaaS products and internal APIs.

### Reason
Use models and deterministic logic to decide what should happen next within configured rules and confidence/risk boundaries.

### Act
Execute authorized actions, prepare them for approval, or return a recommendation. Action is part of the architecture, not an afterthought.

## Long-term experience

The business user should think in terms of:

```text
What should this agent do?
What should it know?
What can it access?
What is it allowed to change?
When must a person approve?
How will we measure whether it is working?
```

They should not need to reason about embeddings, function-calling syntax, provider-specific message formats or vector indexes.

## Product moat hypothesis

The defensible layer is not the model API. It is the accumulated platform capability around:

- permission-aware context assembly;
- reusable integrations and tools;
- agent lifecycle/versioning;
- durable executions;
- approval/policy engine;
- business-specific knowledge pipelines;
- evaluations and observability;
- templates and reusable operational patterns;
- enterprise controls and auditability.

## Vision boundary

APOTHEM should integrate with systems of record rather than attempt to replace every ERP/CRM/support platform. It becomes the intelligence/orchestration layer over those systems.
