# Product + Solutions Operating Model

**Status:** Foundation / Draft  
**Project:** APOTHEM AI  
**Canonical domain:** `apothemai.com.br`

## APOTHEM Platform
Reusable multi-tenant product capabilities: agents, knowledge, connections, flow and control.

## APOTHEM Solutions
Customer-specific configuration/integration/implementation built using Platform primitives.

A solution may justify a new primitive when at least one is true: likely reusable across customers, strategically differentiating, required for enterprise readiness, or materially improves the platform architecture.

Track customization debt. A connector/tool/template belongs to platform only after it has a clear contract and ownership model; do not dump project-specific code into shared packages.
