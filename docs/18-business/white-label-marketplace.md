# White-label and Marketplace Direction

**Status:** Foundation / Draft  
**Project:** APOTHEM AI  
**Canonical domain:** `apothemai.com.br`

## White-label
Future customers/vertical products may embed APOTHEM intelligence through API/SDK/components under their own product experience. The core should support tenant isolation, API credentials and branding metadata without making full white-label UI a current requirement.

## Marketplace
Potential assets: agent templates, workflow templates, connectors and tool packs. Marketplace requires versioning, permissions, provenance, review/security and commercial rules; therefore it is an ecosystem phase, not an MVP checkbox.

Vertical products can use **Powered by Apothem AI** while sharing gateway, knowledge, tools, evals and governance.
