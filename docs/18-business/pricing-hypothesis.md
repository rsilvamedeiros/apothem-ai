# Pricing Hypothesis

**Status:** Foundation / Draft  
**Project:** APOTHEM AI  
**Canonical domain:** `apothemai.com.br`

Likely commercial structure: base subscription/platform fee + included usage + overage/usage dimensions, with enterprise contracts and implementation/setup for Solutions.

Potential segmentation:
- Starter/design partner: limited seats/agents/usage;
- Business: more workspaces, connectors, approvals, retention and API;
- Enterprise: SSO, advanced audit/policies, dedicated limits/support, contractual controls;
- Solutions: implementation/integration services plus ongoing platform subscription.

Do not optimize pricing before usage telemetry exists. Build metering now so pricing can evolve without runtime rewrites.
